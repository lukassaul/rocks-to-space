# Research Proposal Template

*Status: Formative template — adapt to the project and institution.*

## Research question and relevance

State a narrow, falsifiable question. Classify its connection to Rocks2Space as **core**, **enabling**, or **adjacent**, following the [research strategy](../../Strategy/README.md).

## Hypothesis and prior evidence

Describe the hypothesis, competing explanations, key published evidence, and what result would disconfirm it. Do not invent references.

## Methods and analysis

Specify materials, controls, sampling, models, uncertainty, data analysis, and reproducibility arrangements.

## Milestones and outputs

Identify a realistic schedule, personnel and resources, datasets, publications, or software. Explain the near-term value independently of any distant dissemination mission.

## Safety, ethics, and decision gates

Document biosafety, ecological, and planetary-protection implications. State stop/go criteria. No research proposal automatically authorizes biological release.

## References and contributors

List verifiable citations, roles, and potential collaborators without implying unconfirmed partnerships.
