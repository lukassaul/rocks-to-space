# Governance and Scientific Independence

*Status: Formative research outline — open for evidence, critique, and contributions.*

## Purpose and scope

Set principles for transparent decision-making, independent critique, international participation, biosafety, and planetary protection.

## Values and authority

Rocks2Space adopts the **responsibility of capability** as a founding value: with the power to influence life's future comes the responsibility to consider it. This motivates investigation and caution, not unilateral authority to disperse life. Scientific contributors need not endorse this philosophical premise.

Separate research decisions from any future deployment decisions. Evidence of technical feasibility would not establish ethical legitimacy. Independent review, planetary-protection requirements, applicable law, and broad international deliberation are essential before any irreversible biological intervention.

## Questions to investigate

Who evaluates research quality and conflicts of interest? How are data, authorship, and dissent handled? What additional international authorization would biological release require?

## Initial work and outputs

Separate scientific inquiry from deployment decisions. No research success automatically authorizes extraterrestrial biological dissemination. Prioritize Earth stewardship and independent review.

## Research standards

Distinguish established findings, hypotheses, and speculation. Record sources, assumptions, limitations, and disconfirming evidence. Favor open, reproducible work and outcomes useful today. Apply relevant biosafety, ecological safeguards, and planetary-protection requirements; nothing here authorizes biological release.

## Contributions welcome

Researchers may add a scoped literature review, a testable hypothesis, a dataset or model, a critical assessment, or a bounded collaborative proposal. See the [research strategy](../../Strategy/README.md) for the distinction between work Rocks2Space leads, collaborates on, and supports.
