# Technology Spinoffs

*Status: Formative research outline — open for evidence, critique, and contributions.*

## Purpose and scope

Identify practical outputs from research on preservation, radiation exposure, rock materials, long-duration autonomy, and trajectories.

## Questions to investigate

Which tools have demonstrated performance beyond a space scenario? What benchmark or field test would establish utility? What are costs and failure modes?

## Initial work and outputs

Maintain an evidence-based portfolio of reusable datasets, open software, methods, and independently validated prototypes.

## Research standards

Distinguish established findings, hypotheses, and speculation. Record sources, assumptions, limitations, and disconfirming evidence. Favor open, reproducible work and outcomes useful today. Apply relevant biosafety, ecological safeguards, and planetary-protection requirements; nothing here authorizes biological release.

## Contributions welcome

Researchers may add a scoped literature review, a testable hypothesis, a dataset or model, a critical assessment, or a bounded collaborative proposal. See the [research strategy](../../Strategy/README.md) for the distinction between work Rocks2Space leads, collaborates on, and supports.
