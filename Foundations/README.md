# Foundations

Rocks2Space's Foundations branch brings together the scientific, philosophical, and ethical questions behind the survival and possible continuity of life beyond Earth.

## Three connected questions

1. **What threatens life?** [Existential Risks and the Survival of Life](Existential_Risks_and_Life_Survival/README.md) examines mechanisms, evidence, consequences, and uncertainty.
2. **What role might intelligence play?** [Life, Gaia, and Humanity's Role](Life_Gaia_and_Humanity/README.md) explores Earth-system feedbacks, the emergence of intelligence, and the responsibilities humans might choose.
3. **What should we do—and refrain from doing?** [Ethics and Planetary Protection](Ethics_and_Planetary_Protection/README.md) develops stewardship across time, protection of Earth and other worlds, and requirements for responsible research.

**Life is vulnerable → intelligence makes deliberate protection possible → that capacity creates responsibility.** Each transition is a question to examine, not a predetermined conclusion.

## Our guiding commitment

**We are responsible to life across time: its deep past, its living present, and its distant future. We seek to consider all three, prioritizing the protection of life today while preserving its possibilities for tomorrow.**

Our research distinguishes evidence, hypotheses, and philosophical interpretations. Every stage should seek valuable science and benefits today, regardless of whether distant aspirations become achievable.
